About RBH

Like many collectors, my watches sit in a physical box while their photos end up scattered across my camera roll. I really wanted a single place to organize everything - photos, specs, invoices, strap combos, service history, a wishlist for future pieces etc. The overall idea is inspired by the Reserve Bank of India, except here, instead of managing monetary reserves, we are vaulting and safeguarding our watches.

Full disclosure: I am a vibe coder 😅 (go easy on me!), but hey, it gets the job done. I wanted this tool to stay completely free for everyone, which is why I hosted it on a free platform.

I know that in today's age, seamless cloud backups with a quick Google login are the norm. Hosting online databases costs money though, and as an independent creator, I do not have the funds for cloud storage. Going offline first was the best option. It takes a little extra effort to manage manual backups, but the upside is that your photos, invoices, and watch details remain strictly on your own device.

When you create a backup file, I recommend using a strong password so your data stays properly encrypted. The app also includes a PIN lock option that secures the site whenever you leave.

For the best experience, I suggest adding it to your home screen as a web app.

RBH is a personal project for individual use. You are welcome to use it for your own collection, but please do not resell or redistribute the app or its design without permission.

Thank you so much for checking out the site. I hope it helps you track and enjoy your collection a bit more!
